Begin in any of five homelands across the four nations. Your start shapes your arrival, your first quest and your first master. It never limits what you can learn.

Bending

Four elements that never play alike

Every element has its own stance, rhythm, range and way of moving. Weave them into one combo and each new element adds power.

The world is your weapon

Water comes from water

Waterbenders draw from real sources: the sea, rivers, lakes, fountains, canals, even a jar in the market. Draw from a basin and its level drops. Bent water carries the colour of where it came from, and splashes back into the world when you let it go.

  • Snow and ice melt into water in your hands.
  • Earthbenders lift real rocks or tear stones out of the ground.
  • Firebenders keep a flame alive in the palm while they explore.
  • Airbenders double jump, glide and race on an air scooter.

      About me

      Why I made this

      A letter from the developer.

      A letter about the Avatar universe

      Personally, I think the Avatar universe has the potential to be one of the best universes in fiction.

      The world design, the character development, the creativity, everything in it has such huge potential. Four nations, each with its own people, its own philosophy and its own way of moving through the world. A story that can make you laugh and break your heart in the same episode. Characters who grow up in front of you.

      So I thought to myself that a proper Avatar game, at least as far as the limits of AI can take it, would be such an amazing idea. Because sometimes, let’s be honest, Avatar: The Last Airbender did not get the treatment it deserved, sadly. Fans have waited years for a game that simply lets them live in that world: walk through Ba Sing Se, sit by the Spirit Oasis, and learn bending from a master who actually makes them practise.

      I’m just one man who built this entire game in two weeks. No studio, no budget, no team. Only a love for this world and a new kind of tool.

      I think that AI will one day be a part of us that we will no longer be able to ignore. And I believe that if we use it for things like this, to build the things we love and share them with the people who love them too, it will be awesome.

      This game is my love letter to the Avatar universe. I hope that when you play it, it feels a little like coming home.

      The developerAvatar: The Four Nations

      How it was made

      Everything in the game is generated by code as you play: the terrain, the cities, the characters and creatures, the bending effects and every sound effect. It runs on TypeScript and three.js in any modern browser, and it was built with the help of AI.
